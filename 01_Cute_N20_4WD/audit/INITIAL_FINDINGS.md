# Cute N20 4WD — baseline CAD findings

Audit date: 2026-10-09 (India). This document describes the **preserved input revision**, not the corrected working CAD. Source references below are to [baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad). All distances are millimetres unless stated otherwise.

The baseline is not ready for manufacture. The findings below were derived independently from source transforms, Boolean cutter intervals and mating patterns. These calculations establish deterministic source defects; they do not substitute for subsequent rendered mesh validation, slicer inspection or a physical fit test.

## Source authority and generated artefacts

- `cute_rover_complete.scad` defines all geometry and assembly placement. It is the authoritative parametric source.
- The 12 files in `parts/` each use that master and call one module. They contain no independently defined part geometry. Changes to the master affect every wrapper.
- `cute_rover_complete.csg`, `cute_rover_complete.stl` and `cute_rover_assembled_preview.png` are historical generated outputs. They cannot supersede the current source, and their filenames do not prove correspondence to it.
- The existing full-assembly STL is unsuitable as a manufacturing parts set: the source assembly includes motor placeholders and assembled components, some of which overlap. Per-part printable geometry and a visualization assembly must be exported separately.
- The previous repository review measured the historical STL at 227.4263 × 172.5 × 48.9263 mm, with one four-face non-manifold edge and five vertex-connected components. Those values are supporting history, **not a fresh measurement in this independent source audit**; the mesh audit should report its own results and hashes. They already differ from the centred 150 × 105 mm current-source chassis. The old output must therefore be compared with freshly exported source geometry rather than accepted as current.
- `Compact_4WD_Smart_Rover.md` supplies design intent, not frozen component drawings. It says 12 mm wheel width and deck height approximately 42–45 mm, whereas the master sets 14 mm and 38 mm. The document's `compact_rover_top.svg` image reference has no corresponding input file in the Cute directory.
- The requested task file exists as `goal.md`; no `goad.md` was found. Its supplied text ends at the incomplete item `9. Identify parts`, so no further criteria can be recovered from that trailing sentence.

## Confirmed geometry and assembly defects

| ID | Baseline defect and evidence | Technically justified correction |
|---|---|---|
| C01 | **Base starts at the wrong datum.** `rounded_box(center=true)` centres X/Y only and starts at Z=0. The extra translation at [line 63](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L63) puts the base at Z=2..6. The nominal wheel lower envelope is 26.5−22.5=4, so the base underside is 2 mm below the nominal wheel-ground plane. Tread grooves slightly change the precise tyre contact point but do not resolve this defect. | Use one explicit chassis datum; for a base underside at Z=0 and ground clearance G, shaft Z must be wheel radius minus G. Recompute every placed part from mating surfaces. |
| C02 | **Base M3 holes are blind.** Cutters at [line 69](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L69) span Z=−1..5 while the base top is 6, leaving a 1 mm roof. | Extend cutters beyond both faces of the corrected base. |
| C03 | **Base cable slots do not pass through the plate.** The centred cube at [line 73](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L73) spans Z=−4..2, touching only the base underside at 2. | Centre through-cutters on the true plate midpoint with a small overrun beyond each face. |
| C04 | **Motor brackets intersect the base.** The bracket solid at [line 84](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L84) starts at 4 while the base extends to 6. Each bracket is therefore buried in the plate by 2 mm. | Export a local flange that mates to the base top, then place it explicitly in assembly. |
| C05 | **Motor flange holes are blind and do not match the chassis.** Local holes at X=±10,Y=0 span Z=3..10 inside a bracket at Z=4..29 ([line 102](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L102)). Assembly maps them to X=±42/±62,Y=±42.5. None matches the base grid X=−60,−30,0,30,60 and Y=−35,0,35. No accessible screw seat, nut pocket or motor restraint is defined. | Use one shared flange-hole array; limit holes to an accessible flange, provide head/nut access and specify a retention mechanism. |
| C06 | **Motor opening is asymmetric and not mirrored.** The centred insertion cutter at [line 98](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L98) spans X=−16..0,Y=−22..0,Z=8..24. Every bracket receives the same transform at [line 262](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L262), despite comments claiming opposite-side mirroring. | Define local inward/outward directions, provide a deliberate insertion route and mirror side-specific placement. |
| C07 | **Wheel tyre overlaps the chassis by 1 mm laterally.** Wheel centre Y=±58.5; half tyre width 7 gives inner face at ±51.5 versus base edge ±52.5 ([line 33](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L33)). The lower wheel arc overlaps the plate at its Z=4..6 range. | Derive wheel Y from measured gearbox face, shaft length, hub offset and moving clearance. |
| C08 | **Gearbox intersects the integral wheel hub.** For the positive side, motor centre 42.5 gives gearbox Y=55..61; wheel centre 58.5 gives hub Y=50.5..66.5. The Ø12 gearbox intersects wheel material around its Ø3.2 bore. Shaft Y=61..69 engages only 5.5 mm of that 16 mm hub span ([lines 117–123](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L117), [line 133](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L133)). The negative side is symmetric. | Place the wheel outside the gearbox and explicitly compute usable shaft engagement. Confirm actual dimensions before releasing the coupling. |
| C09 | **Wheel coupling does not define torque or axial retention.** The Ø3.2 round wheel bore on nominal Ø3 shaft has neither a matching shaft flat, clamp, radial set screw nor specified interference ([line 136](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L136)). | Design a positive torque and axial lock against the selected shaft; use a measured flat or suitable secured hub. |
| C10 | **Hub adapter is two disconnected halves and has no mating wheel interface.** The full-height 1.2 mm diametric slot crosses the Ø14 adapter ([line 151](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L151)). Axial M3 holes at X=±5 do not clamp those halves. A Ø14 adapter cannot enter the wheel's Ø3.2 bore, and the wheel has no matching bolt pattern or receiving pocket. The adapter is omitted from assembly. | Use a connected hub body with radial locking and an explicit matching wheel pocket/flange/fastener pattern. Leave the radial screw and driver accessible after assembly. |
| C11 | **Battery tray has no floor.** Tray Z=0..7 minus the centred cavity at Z=3 produces cavity Z=−1..7 ([line 162](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L162)), removing the entire bottom. | Start the cavity above a real floor; add secure restraint and cable exit. |
| C12 | **Battery screws lie in the cavity.** Centres X=±29,Y=±15 lie within the opening X=±31,Y=±16 ([line 163](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L163)). The radius 1.6 holes only nibble the Y rim by 0.6 mm, producing edge fragments rather than useful mounting holes. No chassis pattern matches their assembly placement. | Place holes in material outside the cavity, provide head/nut access and share the tab pattern with the base. |
| C13 | **Battery placement is tied to the wrong datum.** Tray bottom 6 merely touches the erroneous base top 6 ([line 275](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L275)). Correcting the base alone to 0..4 would introduce a 2 mm gap. | Constrain tray bottom to corrected base top; derive the pack envelope independently. |
| C14 | **Standoffs miss the deck and chassis holes.** They occupy Z=4..36 but deck underside is 38, leaving 2 mm gap. XY centres ±50/±30 have no matches in the base grid ([lines 209–219](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L209)). | Use shared corner coordinates for both plates and derive spacer height from their actual mating faces. |
| C15 | **Standoffs intersect motor-bracket flanges.** Radius 4 standoffs centred at Y=±30 reach ±34; nearest bracket edges lie at ±32.5. Their solids overlap by up to 1.5 mm laterally in the Z=4..8 flange range. Bracket insertion openings above 8 do not eliminate the flange collision. | Relocate the support or bracket interfaces and check the complete solids, including nuts and tool envelopes. |
| C16 | **Deck cable opening leaves a 1.5 mm roof.** Deck Z=38..41; centred cutter at 37 with height 5 spans 34.5..39.5 ([line 206](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L206)). | Through-cut the deck and coordinate its location with the mounted carriers. |
| C17 | **ESP carrier holes have no deck mates.** Carrier placement at (22,12,41) maps its holes to (−1,0),(−1,24),(45,0),(45,24) ([line 283](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L283)); deck has only four corner holes. Correcting the 45×30 cable aperture would place (−1,0) in empty space. | Add shared mounting holes and move the aperture or carrier pattern together; reserve underside PCB clearance. |
| C18 | **Camera fixture floats.** Bottom Z=42 versus deck top 41 gives 1 mm gap ([line 291](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L291)). No shared deck holes exist. The alternative `camera_front_mount()` is defined but not called. | Use a seated bracket with a real attachment path and forward orientation. |
| C19 | **Front hardware is on the lateral side.** Chassis front/rear is declared X and wheel shafts are along Y, so rolling direction is ±X. Camera, sensor bar and bumper are placed on −Y ([lines 227, 243, 288, 291](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L227)). | Adopt one convention, placing front hardware at −X and aiming sensor optics along the forward axis. |
| C20 | **Bumper is detached and collides with tyres.** Bumper Y=−61.5..−53.5 versus chassis min −52.5 gives 1 mm gap. Its X=±60,Z=10..26 occupies both negative-Y wheel envelopes ([line 243](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L243)). It has no mating tabs. | Move to the front, add fastened tabs and verify tyre rotational clearance. |
| C21 | **ToF bar intersects motor brackets.** Bar X=−43..43,Y=−55.5..−41.5,Z=6..14 overlaps the negative-Y brackets at X=−67..−37 and 37..67,Y=−52.5..−32.5,Z=4..29. Interference is definite at Z=6..8; the negative-X bracket remains solid through additional overlapping regions ([line 288](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L288)). | Place the sensor assembly at the true front and establish clearance to the motors and bracket flanges. |
| C22 | **Sensor bar has no PCB attachment or established forward optics.** Three Ø8 through-Z apertures and two M3 holes do not define retention, module footprint or left/right angles ([line 188](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L188)). Chassis holes do not match it. | Separate carrier-to-chassis fastening from board-specific mounting and optical clearances. |
| C23 | **Printable modules retain assembly transforms.** Deck starts Z=38; bumper Z=10; wheel and adapter cross negative Z; standoffs export four already positioned bodies ([lines 239, 243](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L239)). | Define each printable part at a practical local bed datum and apply placement only inside assembly. Export quantities and orientations explicitly. |
| C24 | **Documentation and loop counts disagree.** The comment says six tread grooves; `0:30:330` creates twelve. Source wheel width14 and deck underside38 differ from the 12 mm and 42–45 mm design notes ([line 139](baseline/cute_rover_complete.scad#L139)). | Update documentation to the final source parameters and identify which dimensions remain provisional. |

## Unresolved component and production requirements

These are selection/measurement questions, separate from confirmed mathematical defects. A family label or generic placeholder cannot establish compatibility.

| Interface | What is available | What is still needed |
|---|---|---|
| N20 motor body and gearbox | Generic body length 25, width 12, height 10; generic gearbox length 6 and diameter 12. Height is unused in baseline geometry. | Exact purchased SKU and mechanical drawing; can and gearbox cross-sections, rounded corners, lengths, shaft eccentricity and wire/terminal exits. The nominal 12×10 rectangular envelope has diagonal 15.62, larger than the baseline Ø13.5 cavity, so that rectangular envelope cannot fit; actual rounded profiles remain unverified. |
| Motor retention | No manufacturer hole drawing or tested clamp is supplied. | Permitted mounting/strap/clamp locations and acceptable compression; fastener sizes, thread depth and terminal access. A generic open saddle with zip ties can be documented as provisional retention, not certified motor-specific fit. |
| Shaft/wheel/hub | Nominal Ø3 shaft and Ø3.2 wheel bore; no flat or usable shaft length drawing. | Shaft actual diameter, flat depth/orientation, exposed length and shoulder; engagement length, locking screw access and axial retention. A radial set screw must remain accessible through an exposed collar or aligned wheel window. |
| Battery | 2S planning baseline; tray opening 62×32 and 7 mm overall height. | Exact chemistry/SKU, L/W/H, mass, connector and cable exit envelopes, restraint and removal route. Neither capacity nor pack height can be inferred from this cavity. |
| Controller/camera/drivers | ESP32-S3, camera and two DRV8833 modules are planned; generic tray/slot geometry only. | Exact selected boards, mounting-hole coordinates, solder/header/USB/camera clearance, cable bends, all driver/regulator mounting and electrical enclosure space. Historical root BOM alternatives do not freeze the Cute hardware. |
| ToF modules | Three VL53L0X breakouts planned. | Selected breakout outline/hole sizes and pitch, optical centres, connector exits and intended side sensor angles. Do not infer all breakout boards share mounting patterns. |
| Power switch and harness | Planned in the design notes. | Actual switch dimensions/fastening, connector envelopes, strain relief, and safe routing outside tyre swept volumes. |
| Fasteners | Nominal3.2 mm M3 clearance; broad M2/M3 planning BOM. | Actual head/nut/washer dimensions, lengths from final stack heights, thread/insert method, tool approach and screw protrusion versus ground clearance. |
| FDM fit and material | PETG/PLA prototype intent, optional TPU tyres/bumper; no calibrated printer. | Nozzle/layer settings, shrink/clearance coupon results, shaft and hole fit, insert/set-screw thread method, minimum load-bearing ligaments, practical support/orientation settings and a slicer check. |
| Structural/performance acceptance | Chassis150×105, nominal45 mm tyres, four motors. | Total mass/centre of gravity, bracket torque loads, wheel radial loads and traction; representative assembly and spin tests. Source geometry alone cannot establish load capacity. |

## Required follow-through

Corrections should preserve the input under `audit/baseline/`, establish shared mating parameters, and export locally oriented individual meshes. Validate every fresh mesh, compare it with its authoritative source and record dimensions and hashes. A visualization assembly should include separately identified motor/component proxies and must never serve as a one-piece manufacturing release.

Even after deterministic defects are corrected, the design remains a **provisional generic prototype** until the unresolved drivetrain and purchased-component dimensions are frozen and representative fit tests succeed.
